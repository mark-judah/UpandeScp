# Logging in and the two surfaces

## Getting in

Log in to the site as normal. You land in Desk.

From the app sidebar choose **Upande SCP**. The top of that sidebar is your way
into the SCP app — click **Dashboards**, **Scouting Map**, **Application Plan**,
**Approvals** or **Chemical Dashboard** and you leave Desk.

Once you are in the SCP app, stay there. Its own sidebar gets you between views
without going back through Desk.

> **The Desk links always open the rose version.** They are fixed to
> `#/rose/...`. On an avocado or coffee farm, use the crop switcher inside the
> app, or edit the crop in the address bar, once you are in.

## What you see depends on your role

The SCP app hides what does not apply to you. Two cases are worth knowing:

**Chemical Store Keepers see only the Stores section.** If you hold the SCP
Chemical Store Keeper role, your sidebar shows **Spray Plan Transfers, Chemical
Dashboard, Labels** and **Chemical Progress** — and nothing else. That is
deliberate, not a fault. Everything else in the app is hidden for that role.

**Everyone else sees the Stores section hidden**, and gets Overview, Scouting,
Crop Protection and Reports instead.

If a colleague describes a screen you cannot find, compare roles before
assuming it is broken.

## The seven SCP roles

| Role | Broadly |
|---|---|
| SCP Scout | Records observations in the field |
| SCP Scouting User | Reads scouting data |
| SCP Spray Plan Creator | Builds spray plans |
| SCP Spray Plan Approver | Approves or rejects them |
| SCP Spray Supervisor | Runs the spray on the day, declares postponements |
| SCP Chemical Store Keeper | Issues chemicals, prints labels, tracks stock |
| SCP General Manager | Oversight across the whole lifecycle |

Who can do what precisely is in the admin book,
[Roles and permissions](/scp/admin/roles-and-permissions).

## When to go back to Desk

Only to look at or change the reference data behind the app:

| You want to | Desk sidebar |
|---|---|
| Add a pest, disease, weed, predator, incident or disorder | Observations & Threats |
| Add a greenhouse, bed or zone, or change map settings | Farm & Infrastructure |
| Look up a chemical, or a FRAC/IRAC/GHS code | Chemistry |
| See who is a scout, sprayer or supervisor | Teams |
| Open a spray plan as a raw document | Applications → Application Floor Plans |

Most of those are administrator jobs. They are covered in the
[admin book](/scp/admin).
