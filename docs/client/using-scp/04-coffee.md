# Scouting coffee

`/scp_app#/coffee/…`

Coffee is **set up but not yet in use**. Its reference data is configured and
its views exist; no coffee has been scouted yet.

Read this chapter as "what will be there when coffee scouting starts", not as a
description of something running.

## Where you will scout

The same block / row / tree structure as avocado, rather than the greenhouse /
bed / zone structure roses use.

## What is already configured

Coffee has the **largest pest list of the three crops** — 16, against 12 each
for roses and avocado.

**16 pests**

Antestia Bug · Berry Borer · Berry Moth · Brown Scale · Capsid Bug ·
Coffee Thrips · Fruit fly (Ceratitis) · Green Scale · Kenya Mealybug · Lace Bug ·
Leaf Miner · Leaf Skeletonizer · Sting Caterpillar · Systates Weevil ·
Tailed Caterpillar · Yellow Termites

**5 diseases**

Bacterial Blight of Coffee · Brown Eye Spot · Coffee Berry Disease ·
Coffee Leaf Rust · Coffee Wilt (Fusarium)

Every one carries its own thresholds and stages, so recording can begin without
further configuration.

## The views you have

| Section | Views |
|---|---|
| Overview | Dashboards |
| Scouting | Scouting Map |
| Reports | Reports |

That is the whole of it — three views.

## What coffee does not have

**No Trends.** No Observations list, no Heatmaps, no Traps view. None of the
crop-protection chain — no Jobsheet, no Application Plan, no Approvals.

So coffee today is: record entries on the map, look at the dashboard, run the
weekly report.

## Before the first real scouting round

Worth confirming with your administrator:

1. That the blocks, rows and trees are set up for the coffee estate.
2. That the pest and disease **thresholds** suit your farm — they are configured
   but have never been tested against real counts.
3. Which stages scouts are expected to record for each pest.

The first weeks of coffee data are also the first test of that configuration.
Expect to tune the thresholds once real numbers arrive.
