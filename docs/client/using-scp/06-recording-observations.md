# Recording observations

Every field observation becomes a **Scouting Entry**, numbered `SCE-YYYY-…`.

Scouts normally capture these on a mobile device in the field. This chapter
describes what a scouting entry contains, so that anyone reading one — or
checking one in Desk — knows what they are looking at.

## What every entry carries

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Scout** | Required. The Employee who recorded it |
| **Date and time of capture** | Both required |
| **Latitude and longitude** | Both required — every observation is a point on the map |
| **Crop scouted** | Which crop this entry is about |
| **Location** | Greenhouse / bed / zone for roses; block / row / tree for avocado and coffee |

The location and the coordinates are what let the same record appear on the map,
in a heatmap, and in a report.

## What can be recorded in one entry

A single entry can carry any mix of eight kinds of observation, each as its own
list of findings:

| Kind | What it records |
|---|---|
| **Pests** | The pest, its stage, and severity |
| **Diseases** | The disease, its stage, and severity |
| **Predators** | Beneficial insects present |
| **Weeds** | Weeds found |
| **Incidents** | Anything out of the ordinary that is not a pest or disease |
| **Physiological disorders** | Non-biological problems — nutrient, water, environmental |
| **Traps** | Trap counts |
| **Crop modelling** | Growth-stage and crop-development observations |

You do not have to fill in all of them. Record what you found.

## Stages matter

Pests, diseases and predators are recorded **with a stage**, not just a name —
egg, larva, adult; fresh, dry, latent. The stage is what makes the record
actionable: the same pest at two stages calls for two different responses, and
the reports and heatmaps read the stage as well as the count.

The stages available for each pest or disease, and the order they appear in, are
configured by your administrator per plant part. If a stage you expect is
missing, that is a configuration question, not a bug in the app.

## Predators are not a problem

Predators are the beneficial insects. Recording them is recording something
good — a healthy predator population is the reason not to spray. Do not skip
them because nothing is wrong.

## Reviewing what has been recorded

**Observations** in the sidebar lists the entries themselves, so you can read
back the detail behind a coloured cell on the map. **Trends** shows how a pest
or disease has moved over time, and **Heatmaps** shows where pressure sits
across the farm.

Administrators can also open Scouting Entries directly in Desk, though there is
rarely a reason to.
