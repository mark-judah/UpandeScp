# Dashboards and trends

Two views under **Overview** in the SCP app sidebar. Both are read-only.

## Dashboards

`/scp_app#/<crop>/dashboard`

The landing view, and the one the Desk sidebar's **Dashboards** link opens. It
summarises the current state of the crop: what is being found, where the
pressure is, and what is happening with spraying.

Every crop has a dashboard — this is the one view that exists for roses,
avocado and coffee alike.

## Trends

`/scp_app#/<crop>/trends`

Movement over time rather than a snapshot: how a pest or disease has risen or
fallen week by week.

Available for roses and avocado. **Coffee has no trends view.**

## Reading them honestly

Both views are built from scouting entries. That means they show **what was
recorded**, not what was present. A block nobody scouted this week is not a
clean block — it is an unscouted one, and it will look quiet on every chart in
the app.

When a dashboard shows an unexpected drop, check scouting coverage before
concluding the pressure fell.

## Reports

The **Reports** view, at the bottom of the sidebar for every crop, is where the
periodic reports live — including the weekly block reports for avocado and
coffee. Reports are generated from the same scouting data and can be exported.
