Coffee coverage is not measurable yet
Coffee has no location hierarchy in the system. Roses report against beds and zones, avocado against blocks and rows — coffee has estate, section and triad on paper and nothing to record against in software, so there is no denominator to divide by.
Until that model exists this email carries what can be counted: entries made, and what the scouts saw. Coverage, cost per unit and the spray programme follow once coffee has a shape to hang them on.
