Crop icons

Cut out of the one sheet into their own files so they can be used anywhere — three tints each (accent, light, ink) at 512px and 96px, in docs/design/icons/. The masthead of each email carries its crop's icon on an accent chip instead of the crop's name.

Charts are the one thing still to come: production renders them server-side as PNG and attaches them with cid:, since Gmail strips SVG. The bar rows here are already email-safe.
